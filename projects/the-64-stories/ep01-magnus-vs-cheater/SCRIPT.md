# EP01 — Stockfish Couldn't Beat Him (VO script, Callum)

This guy challenged Magnus Carlsen to a live game. But he wasn't alone.
Aleksandar. Rated twenty-one eighteen.
Magnus? Twenty-eight ninety-two. World champion.
This should be over in twenty moves.
But move after move, Aleksandar plays perfectly. Not good. Perfect.
Because someone was whispering in his ear. Stockfish. The strongest chess engine on Earth.
Even Magnus noticed.
[REAL CLIP — Magnus, original audio: "Yeah, this guy's playing really well."]
So Magnus stopped attacking. He traded queens. Then rooks. And dragged him into a long, slow endgame.
Because Stockfish doesn't move the pieces. A human does. And copying every move takes time.
Magnus had over a minute. Aleksandar had seconds.
Ten. Five. Zero.
Time's up. Magnus wins.
Aleksandar was later accused of using Stockfish. Which means the strongest engine on Earth just lost to Magnus.
Savage.
