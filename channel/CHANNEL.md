# The Rule Stories — channel copy

## Description (YouTube "About", ~900 characters)

Every story here comes with rules.

The Rule Stories are animated survival horror stories told to you, in second person, one night at a time. Every phone on Earth rings at 3:00 AM. The stores are open but nobody is working. A notebook in your grandmother's drawer is dated 1987. And somewhere in the middle of it, there's a list. Follow it, and you might make it to morning.

Each episode is one situation, told night by night, with the rules you'd need to survive it. Some of them are pure fiction. Some of them are real advice you can use today. Part of the fun is figuring out which is which.

New stories every week.
Watch to the end: the last rule is never what you expect.

And when you think you've figured out the next rule, write it in the comments. Someone is always listening.

## Delivery checklist (every episode, before sending or uploading)

1. Audio stream duration == video duration (±0.1 s): `ffprobe -show_entries stream=codec_type,duration`.
2. Speech recognised at the start, middle, last minute and last 15 s of the delivered file.
3. Loudness −14 LUFS integrated, true peak ≤ −1 dBTP, measured on the delivered file, not the mix.
4. Contact sheet of the delivered file (1 frame / 25 s) reviewed for clipped text and empty frames.
5. Never use `-shortest` when muxing a raw stream; cut with an explicit `-t <video duration>`.
