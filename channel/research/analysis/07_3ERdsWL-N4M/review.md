## 10. Top 10 reusable techniques

1. Thumbnail-as-first-frame: open on the thumbnail composition with a red alarm flicker and a fast
   +16% push, then cut into the story.
2. In-scene rule slam: number badge + "RULE" + black slab flies in rotated/oversized, settles
   top-centre, then docks top-left as a persistent mini label.
3. Docking HUD elements: day plate and clock appear huge centre-frame, then shrink to a corner and
   stay — every new state is announced, then becomes chrome.
4. "Movie hero vs you" split-screen comparison as an early relatability beat.
5. Growing checklists with tick/X on the right side of a static shot (shop list, wound care,
   "time · place · order") to fill long holds.
6. White 2-4 frame flash = time skip / rule; red full-frame flash = death.
7. Very slow push-ins (~0.15%/s) to carry 20-30 s holds; big slow pull-outs (-10 to -19%) for
   reveals.
8. Data diagrams for survival logic (floors coloured by danger, water litres per day, cell tower
   calls vs SMS, route map with fallback).
9. End recap: all rules re-listed in two columns as the narrator recites them, last rule in red.
10. Twist question floating in empty sky ("whose voice was it at 3 a.m.?") right before the CTA.
