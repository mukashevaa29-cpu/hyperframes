A different format from the "N rules" explainers: a **first-person creepypasta** (night watchman Андрей, 6 rules plus a
torn-off 7th). It is the slowest edit in 17-20: **65 shots, 4.7 cuts/min, median shot 13.2 s** (p10 3.8 / p90 20.8 s).

dark: **median luma 38, 52 % of frames below luma 40**, palette #2a2a30 / #1f1e22 / #100c10 with an accent of blood-red
#530f0e. Loudness −18.6 LUFS, LRA 7.6, momentary p10 −35.5 (long quiet passages).

## 9. Pacing

Something new every ~1.5 s (17 pop-ins + 41 captions per minute), but **one image per 13 s**. Tension comes from holding
a sparse stage while the voice reads a quote. Each night follows the same pattern: night card (15 s) → 3–6 shots of the
incident → the anomaly reveal on a prop (logbook, photo, handprints) → the lesson tag → the next night card. That is
about 1:30 per night.

## 10. Top 10 reusable techniques

1. **Held "Night N" card** with a giant red numeral, the rule slab and the paper sheet highlight, kept on screen for the
   whole rule explanation.
2. **Thumbnail-as-cold-open frame** (the first 5 s literally restate the thumbnail).
3. **Missing final rule** as the hook ("ПРАВИЛО №7 ОТОРВАНО"), paid off by unfolding the strip.
4. **Prop-based reveals**: the logbook "in my handwriting", handprints on the inside of the glass, the guard roster
   through 2026.
5. **Full-frame blood-red tint** for 1–3 s on the scare line.
6. **Flashlight wedge plus wall ellipse** as the only light source in a corridor.
7. **Empty-stage open**: cut to a near-black stage with only the caption, then fade the object in.
8. **5.8 % entry settle** on every hard cut, then a very slow late push (0.5–1 %/s).
9. **Fourth-wall closer** ("ТЫ ВЕДЬ НЕ ЗВОНИЛ ПО ТОМУ ОБЪЯВЛЕНИЮ?") before the CTA.
10. **Moon-phase progress row** (7 moons, the last red) instead of a HUD.

## Craft mistakes not to copy

- Long stretches of near-black frames (52 % below luma 40) with tiny props in the centre. On a phone in daylight it is
  a black screen with captions. Lift the stage to at least luma 30–40 and make the key prop bigger than 20 % of the frame.
- Captions fade up together with the shot, so the first 5–10 frames of a caption are grey-on-black and unreadable (rows
  0004, 0021).
- Some cards and tags sit at ~2 % height ("ПОСЛЕ ЛИФТА", the sheet text). The rules sheet itself is illegible at any size.
- The red night numeral is a different style from the rule numerals of 17/18/20 (italic block versus rounded). Choose one
  numeral style per channel.
- 15–19 s static holds on the night card with only small slab changes are too long. The few seconds that
  work are the ones where the sheet slides in.
