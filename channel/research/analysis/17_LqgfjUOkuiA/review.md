## 4. Light & shadow (how the bright look is built)

- **Day/night ≈ 85/15.** Daylight is built from: a sky gradient from light grey-blue (#dbe2e8 at the top) to a white haze at
  the horizon; **one large soft white bloom** in a top corner (usually top-right) as the sun; a sea band of teal (#1c6b8c
  to #44718b) with thin lighter dash strokes; ochre sand (#c3a98c / #b0704c); soft white cumulus blobs. Shadows are only
  a darker sand ellipse under figures.
- **The vignette stays heavy in daylight** (median 52, up to 90 at the corners): warm-brown corners frame every bright
  plate, so day scenes still read as "cinematic" and keep captions legible. Grain plus slowly drifting white dust specks
  are on top.
- Time is coded by the colour of the sky: morning cyan-white → **evening pink-violet gradient** (#c77aa0 → #6a4e8a) for the
  calm before the disaster → **after the wave the sky goes flat grey-white and the ground brown** (#5d5143 is the
  second-largest palette share) → night navy-black with a moon disc → dawn orange band.
- Tension is drawn with **colour, not darkness**: the water turns brown, the sky desaturates, and big red text sits on
  white ("ГУЛ ВОЗВРАЩАЕТСЯ" 19:18). Light events: 72 darken / 8 flash / 7 brighten, mostly overlay fades. The flashes are
  on quake beats (5:00) and the wave impact (14:48).

## 6. Camera (shots.json + frames.npz)

- **Push-in on 59 % of real shots** (115/194). Static 87, pull-out 13, pans 39 (left 23, right 16: lateral drifts along
  the flood), shake 37 (value median 1.6, the highest of 17-20: quake and wave scenes).
- **Push profile:** total scale median **+5.5 %** (p10 +3.5, p90 +14.5 %). **Hold first, then push:** the move
  reaches 10 % of its travel at a median 49 % of the shot and 90 % at 80 %. Active speed median **1.8 %/s** (p10 0.7,
  p90 3.6 %/s). Easing is mostly ease-out (75 of 111) and it lands ~1–2 s before the cut.
- **Origin:** biased to the lower half where the characters stand: bottom-right 40, bottom-left 18, bottom-centre 16,
  top 24, middle 13. The pan component is up to ±23 px at 480 px width, so pushes are diagonal towards the subject.
- **Chaining:** runs of up to 10 consecutive push-in shots. Every cut resets to 100 % and the next shot pushes again,
  giving a saw-tooth of slow pushes for the whole film.
- **Entry settle:** 87 % of shots >=2 s show a scale change of more than 1.5 % in the first 0.8 s. Measured with ORB
  against the frame at +0.7 s, the new shot starts **2–11 % larger and settles to 100 % within 0.1–0.3 s**. Most of this
  hides inside the cross-dissolve.
- Pull-outs (zoom 0.92–0.97 over 0.8–5.8 s) reveal scale: crowd, flood extent, hotel.

## 9. Pacing

typical 6–7 s shot: dissolve in with a settle, then 3–6 tags on the spoken nouns, then a slow push from mid-shot, then a
dissolve out. The story is told on an explicit clock (9:31 → 9:40 → 10:00 → 12:00 → evening → dawn), and the HUD clock

## 10. Top 10 reusable techniques

1. **Story-prop rule card** (rules on the back of the hotel card): a cream paper card with a red circled numeral and a
   tilted navy slab, ~1.6 s, slide-wiped in and out.
2. **Theme-skinned chapter card** (lifeguard signboard) that **changes material after the turning point** (red → wet
   brown).
3. **State-meter HUD** ("МОРЕ: СПОКОЙНО → УХОДИТ → ВОЛНА → НА КРЫШЕ: 40") beside the story clock: a free tension gauge.
4. **Hold-then-push camera**: still for half the shot while tags land, then +3–9 % ease-out at ~1.8 %/s towards the
   lower-half subject. Reset on every cut.
5. **Micro-dissolve (4–6 frames) plus a 2–6 % entry settle** as the default join.
6. **Bright day built with vignette kept**: pale sky gradient, a corner sun bloom, teal sea dashes, ochre sand, brown
   corner vignette.
7. **Colour-coded time of day** (cyan → pink dusk → grey-brown aftermath → navy night → orange dawn) as the act structure.
8. **Infographic interludes on a white void** (cross-sections, bar charts, icon rows) every 1–2 min to teach the rule.
9. **Mid-roll A/B question card** ("ХОЛМ ИЛИ ГОСТИНИЦА? ПИШИ В КОММЕНТАРИЯХ").
10. **Callback ending**: the old man's flashback on the same beach plate with a sepia tint, the full rule list on one
    sheet, and the 20th rule written by hand.

## Craft mistakes not to copy

- **Captions colliding with diagrams and HUD:** the caption runs over the red X and the crowd (frame 0090, "ЕСЛИ ТЫ
  ВИДИШЬ ВОЛНУ ЦУНАМИ" becomes illegible over the sand). On rule cards the caption sits on the bottom progress row.
- **Dissolve ghosting:** two captions and two sets of tags are visible together for 3–5 frames, and the half-faded
  previous tag text reads as clutter (frames 0054, 0225: "ДАЖЕ НЕ СТРАШНО" ghosting).
- **Too many simultaneous tags** (up to 6) at ~2.5 % height. Some are clipped by the frame edge (the Miша inset at 0010
  cuts "ВО…" off at the right) and some duplicate the caption.
- **Over-reused plates:** the uphill road with traffic is used for 9 consecutive shots (7:15–7:48) with only overlays
  changing. It reads as a slideshow.
- The HUD rule dots are ~13 px tall at 540p and unreadable on mobile. The HUD has 3 elements plus tags competing in the
  top 15 %.
- Captions are split by count, not meaning ("ПОЛОСА МОКРОГО ПЕСКА РАСТЁТ НА", "ПРИБОЙ, ВЫШКУ НОМЕР ТРИ ЛОМАЕТ,").
- Rules without a card (only spoken) break the 1–20 rhythm that the HUD promises.
