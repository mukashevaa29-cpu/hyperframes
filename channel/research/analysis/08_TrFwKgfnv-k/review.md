## 4. Light & shadow

- Day ~50%, sunset ~8%, night ~25%, black cards ~17%. Overcast grey-blue daylight dominates
  (desaturated: sat median 66.5).
- Light sources and how drawn: street lamps = large soft circular halos (~180 px dia at 960 w,
  ~35% opacity warm grey) — whole circles, not cones; searchlights = long translucent white
  trapezoid beams crossing in the sky (moving); window lamp = bright radial glow with curtain stripes
  (10:30); phone/torch light = soft circle around the character (14:45 "lights himself with the
  phone"); "sun cuts their eyes" = a white sun disc with thin radiating lines (8:06).

  crosshair, 2:24), door peephole (soft circular iris, `shots/0034.jpg`), binoculars (oval iris,
  `shots/0064.jpg`), spotlight on a bandage (12:57). Everything outside the circle is ~90% black.

  e.g. 229.2, 706.3, 727.2, 770.5, 1018.7, 1120.7, 1139.9).

- Temperature: cool grey daytime; warm only at sunset (4:12, 8:30, pink-orange sky #e3906a) and the

## 10. Top 10 reusable techniques

1. MacGuffin spine: a box you must not open, set up in the first 20 s and paid off as a
   series cliffhanger ("in episode 2 — what's in the box") in the CTA.
2. Rule card build in ~1 s on a streaked black card (numeral slide + "RULE" + tilted yellow slab +
   progress dots) with the 13th/last rule hidden as a blank dot until it is earned, then yellow.
3. Circular iris masks for POV (sniper scope, peephole, binoculars, spotlight).
4. Visualised sound: green waveform = alive, red/grey = recording/foreign; radios with rings;
   stuck-record vinyl as a rule metaphor.
5. Repetition shown by duplicated speech bubbles around one speaker.
6. Colour-coded cast (yellow straps, teal scrubs, yellow raincoat) + names in yellow in captions.
7. Giant tilted red onomatopoeia/quantity words (БАМ, ВЫСТРЕЛ, ДЕСЯТКИ, СОТНИ) as event beats.
8. Explainer diagrams between beats (message timeline, face graph for food sharing, infection timer
   on a plaster, building section).
9. Big reveal camera moves reserved for 2-3 moments (-25% pull-out + pan on the prospect, +36%
   push-in + tilt to the antenna).
10. Twist via a dated artefact (blackboard dated 14.10.2023: "nobody is coming") + a choice CTA
    ("give, open or leave?").
