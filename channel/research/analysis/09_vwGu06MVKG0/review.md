## 10. Top 10 reusable techniques

1. **A serialized micro-story spine:** one building, 3-4 recurring neighbours, a day counter. Every rule is triggered by a story incident and its consequence is shown afterwards (rule = moral of the episode).
2. **The "ПОЧЕМУ." pivot:** a one-word caption on an empty dark stage, then a build-up diagram. A clean, repeatable switch from story mode to science mode.
3. **Light level as plot progress:** the same stairwell and room are re-shown progressively darker as days pass (luma 130 -> 46). Ch4 "ТЕМНОТА" turns off windows block by block.
4. **A 21-s cold open:** a mundane trigger, "НЕ ВЕРЬ", a day counter, the "20" promise ring, then rule 1 before 1:00.
5. **On-screen story clocks:** a dark pill "7-Й ДЕНЬ · ТИШИНА", "4:10" in the scene's top-left to timestamp each vignette.
6. **Body-heat auras:** an orange radial for warmth, a red pulsing glow for alcohol vasodilation, a blue number for the cold core.
7. **Colour-coded symptom stacks:** numbered tags in red -> orange -> purple, popping one per spoken symptom.
8. **Rule-card breath:** 0.3-0.5 s of empty streaked dark stage before the numeral pops, as a palate cleanser.
9. **A white-flash "impact" cut** (0.17 s plus a desaturated wash) for a single violent story event.
10. **An explicit rule callback tag** ("ПОМНИШЬ ПРАВИЛО 7?") to make the rules feel cumulative. The end card asks "which rule will you break first?" over the full 20-dot row.
