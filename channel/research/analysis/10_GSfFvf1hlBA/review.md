## 1. Structure

- **Genre shift vs 01-08: folk-horror serial in second person ("ты").** It is not survival advice. The 13 rules are a dead grandmother's handwritten "ЗИМНИЕ ПРАВИЛА" notebook that the viewer-protagonist finds in her village house. Each rule is a supernatural taboo (don't answer the forest by name, cover mirrors at night, don't build snowmen, count footprints every morning, don't let in anyone without breath steam). Each one gets a rationalised "ПОЧЕМУ ЭТО РАБОТАЕТ" explanation.

## 9. Pacing

- Slow cut rate (5.4/min) but constant additions: 35 pop-ins/min plus 47 caption changes/min. In long wides a bubble, tag or label lands every 1-2 s (shot 53: 25 pop-ins in 9.1 s during the banya explanation).
- Story beats alternate dialogue (bubbles) and narration (captions). Rule cards arrive every 1:00-1:40.
- The tension rhythm uses static shots and darkening rather than cutting faster. Cut rate barely rises at climaxes; instead shakes and short inserts (1.3-2.3 s) are used (12:28, 12:38).

## 10. Top 10 reusable techniques

1. **A diegetic rulebook:** the rules come from a found object (a notebook "ЗИМНИЕ ПРАВИЛА") and the final rule is personalised ("БАБУШКИНО ПРАВИЛО", yellow).
2. **A mystery cold open ending on a question card:** "ТОГДА КТО ПРИСЛАЛ ТЕБЕ СМС?" on black with a shake, ~2:00 in.
3. **A "ПОЧЕМУ ЭТО РАБОТАЕТ" bullet panel** over the live scene, bullets appended per clause, with a red final bullet.
4. **Chapters as nights:** a moon-icon card that rack-focuses in, with a labelled 7-stop timeline.
5. **Dialogue bubbles with yellow tails** for every character line, so the narration captions stay pure narration.
6. **Portrait inserts with a POV hand** in the foreground for "you" moments at doors and windows.
7. **Voice rings:** pale green concentric ellipses to draw sounds and voices calling from outside.
8. **A countdown-insert climax:** "20 / 19 / 18 МИНУТ ДО СОЛНЦА" hard-cut cards.
9. **A comment-vote end card:** a binary question, "от ответа зависит вторая серия", and an animated cursor clicking subscribe with a ring pulse on the bell.
10. **Letters revealed line by line** with the next line ghosted at low opacity, plus a heavy vignette on bright paper.
