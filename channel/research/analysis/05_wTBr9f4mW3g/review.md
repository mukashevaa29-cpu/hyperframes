## 8. Overlays, text & effects

- Captions: always on, bottom-centre, baseline at ~91% of frame height; heavy geometric sans
  (Montserrat Black/ExtraBold-like), ALL CAPS, white with a soft dark drop shadow, cap height
  ~5% of frame height (27 px at 540). 2-6 words per caption (one short clause), single line, max
  ~70% frame width. Not karaoke: the whole phrase swaps at once (46.7 changes/min = one every
  1.3 s). One keyword per caption is recoloured red/yellow/green by meaning (see 5). On rule cards
  the whole "ПРАВИЛО ТРЕТЬЕ" caption is yellow.
- Labels ("stickers"): small rounded-rect tags with heavy caps, 3 styles: white text on black
  (neutral), black text on yellow #f4d63a (fact/keyword), white on red #e62645 (danger), white on
  green (safe). Height ~4-5% of frame, placed top-centre (y ~5-8%) or next to the object, often
  tilted -2 to -4°. Pop: scale from ~0 with small overshoot, ~0.2 s.
- Stamps: outline-only rectangle, rotated ~-8 to -12°, red ("ЕДИНСТВЕННЫЙ НАСЛЕДНИК", "ЛЕТ
  СОРОК", "ПОСЛЕ ЗАКАТА", "ДЛЯ ТОГО, КТО ДЕРЖИТ КАЛИТКУ") or green ("НЕ ОГЛЯДЫВАЙСЯ", "ЕЁ НЕ
  ОБМАНУТЬ"); they slam in large and settle.
- Speech bubbles: black rounded rect with white caps and a small yellow down-pointer; dialogue
  quoted from characters (taxi driver, Tamara, voices from outside).
- Listing pop-ins: labels attached to objects one by one ("КЛУБ «1979»", "КОЛОДЕЦ", "КОЗЫ", ...
  2:15-2:30; 12 labels in 15 s). Numbered list rows with round number badges (3:54-4:09).
- Highlights: yellow outlined rectangle or ring around the object being named; red dashed ellipse
  around a threat; green concentric ellipse ring = a voice coming from that spot.
- Icons/graphics: red X marks over "birds / wind" (2:12), green ticks, calendar tiles, red digital

## 10. Top 10 reusable techniques

1. Two-level progress system: day cards with a 7-node timeline + persistent top-left chapter chip +
   12-dot rule tracker that flips red the moment a rule card lands.
2. Rule card recipe: streaked black bg, giant red numeral sliding in with blur, white "RULE",
   tilted yellow slab with the rule in black caps, 3-4 s hold, slow 1-3% push.
3. Keyword colour code in captions: red = danger/negation, yellow = object/keyword, green = safe.
4. Word-locked tags and stamps (black/yellow/red/green) popping on the spoken noun, ~1 per second.
5. Graphic reset: wipe all elements to the empty dark bg, then rebuild — used before cards and in
   diagrams.
6. Second-person cold open: time stamp + stranger call + inheritance + place missing from maps +
   deadline, all in 78 s with zero explanation.
7. Explainer diagrams between story beats (top-down village map, knock list, well cross-section with
   a sliding sun, branching road graph).
8. Threat grammar: tall black faceless silhouette + red dashed ellipse; ghost = 55% opacity + green
   glow.
9. Heavy vignette + grain + floating dust on flat vector art to make it feel like a film.
10. Twist epilogue that brings a rule home (city door, same knocks, same scratches) followed by a
    question CTA that recaps all 12 dots and an animated cursor clicking Subscribe.
