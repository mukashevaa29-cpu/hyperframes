## 8. Overlays, text & effects

- **Captions (f_160):** white heavy rounded sans, caps, cap height ≈4.6% H, centred at y≈90%, 2-6 words. Keyword colours yellow, red and green. A soft dark shadow, no box.
- **Tags:** rounded rectangles with ≈3% H text, tilted -3° to +3°, popping in with an overshoot (≈6 frames). Fills: yellow #f2c94c with dark text, navy #2f4a7a with white, red #c8203a with white, white with dark text. Placement top-centre or beside the object; 2-4 can stack (f_135.5).
- **Speech bubbles:** black rounded rectangles with white caps and a small tail ("ЭТО НЕ ОН", "ДА, ВСЕ ДОМА"). Dialogue is in bubbles; narration is in captions.

## 9. Pacing

- Long shots (median 11.7 s) filled with 33 pop-ins/min. Shot 90 (43 s) carries 18 pop-ins, and the cemetery shot (32 s) 36.
- Each new element lands on its noun. Tags often **repeat the caption phrase** (caption "НА ПУСТОЙ ТАРЕЛКЕ ЛЕЖИТ КУСОК" plus tag "ЕГО НИКТО НЕ КЛАЛ").
- Rhythm per rule: story (40-90 s) -> 1.3 s pre-roll -> card 3-5 s -> abstract explanation insert (10-25 s) -> back to story.

## 10. Top 10 reusable techniques

1. **A diegetic clock pill under the progress dots**, turning into a red LED countdown near the climax (midnight, then 00:01-00:40).
2. **Zoom-blur transitions plus short dip-to-blacks** for a more cinematic, less "slideshow" feel. Keep hard cuts for rule cards.
3. **A themed chapter icon:** a seasonal ornament (bauble) dropping onto the chapter card, with a matching themed rule-card corner prop (a glowing tree).
4. **A 1.3-s rule pre-roll plate with shake**, then the numeral resolves from a blur; the exit is a horizontal motion smear.
5. **A teaser-first cold open:** the climax image (silhouettes at the door, "НЕ ОТКРЫВАЙ") for 11 s, then a whip to "earlier that day".
6. **Story clock times spoken as one-word yellow captions** plus a clock pill, so the night progresses visibly.
7. **Coloured selection rings** (yellow = notice, red = wrong/danger, green = safe) instead of arrows.
8. **Translucent giant numbers / years behind an object** ("2026 | door | 2027", "1943 / 1956") to encode time.
9. **Cards are optional:** minor rules are delivered as in-scene red tags, and the final rule appears as handwriting on the in-story list.
10. **Daylight held back for the epilogue:** night palette for 21 minutes, then a low-saturation white morning for the twist.
