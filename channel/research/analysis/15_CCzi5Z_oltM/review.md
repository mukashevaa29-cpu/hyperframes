- **Cut timing:** cuts land after the sentence's last word. Many sentences are a single word ("Туман." "Тук." "Шесть."), so cuts often fall on one-word captions.
- **Into every card:** an empty streaked stage for ≈0.3–0.5 s (0064).
- **Fake-cut counter beat (852–860 s):** on a static lamp-room shot, a red counter top-left counts bell strikes 2, 3, 4… 8 with **accelerating intervals (1.0 → 0.6 → 0.5 → 0.4 → 0.3 → 0.2 s)** while a tag "ВСЁ ЧАЩЕ · ВСЁ ГРОМЧЕ" holds. ## 9. Pacing

- **Words:** slow VO (129 wpm) in 1–6 word sentences. A caption changes every ≈1.36 s.
- **Visual events:** one every 1.8 s (33/min). Tags are tightly synced to the nouns. Each spoken item in a list gets its own tag ("КАМНИ · ЧАЙКИ · ВОДОРОСЛИ").
- **Rule interval:** irregular. Rules 1–3 come in 36 s (2:15–2:51), then a 2:15 gap. Nights alternate a quick "procedure" rule with a long incident.
- **Night structure:** (chapter card 2–3 s) → (rule card 3–9 s) → setup at dusk or in the lamp room → incident with a voice, a light or the water → aftermath at dawn (grey-day shot) → next night.

## 10. Top 10 reusable techniques

1. **The password-of-the-day system:** a recurring graphic (avatar card plus a green heavy word plus an icon) that sets up a later scare when a voice gets the word wrong (a red X over the word).
2. **Counter on a held shot:** a big number swaps in sync with the spoken count (steps 115/116/117/**118 red**, bell strikes with accelerating gaps of 1.0 to 0.2 s, reflection counts). Tension without cuts.
3. **The isolation pull-out:** a 13 s pull-out from 1.0 to 0.77 with white edge arrows for "the sea on all sides".
4. **Stacked kinetic title block** in the cold open: big white, yellow, red, a black tag and a yellow outlined tilted stamp, each landing on its phrase.
5. **Rules painted on walls** by different past keepers (mixed colours, years under them). The last rule is in fresh paint with a signature: the found-list device, re-skinned.
6. **The recurring home-base set** (lamp room: a glowing lens, gears, the radio, a desk) that the story returns to every night, with the clock chip changing.
7. **The light ellipse on the water** as a stage: figures appear only inside the beam's footprint.
8. **The journal as a battleground:** lines write themselves in blue, get struck in red, and are closed with a cover-swing wipe.
9. **Full-rule recap card** before the end card (both columns of the 13 rules, 3 s).
10. **Rule header re-pinned in the scene** ("ПРАВИЛО 9" in yellow at top) when an old rule is applied, so the viewer is reminded without a new card.

**Craft mistakes not to copy:**

- The HUD chapter pill text is so small and dim (≈1.6% of frame height, dim yellow) that it is unreadable at phone size, unlike 13/14.
- Chapter card plus rule card back to back (≈6 s of black stage) stalls the momentum at the start of nights 3 and 4.
- The ellipse "spotlight" on the water is a flat grey disc with a hard bottom cut by the boat. It reads as a UI shape, not light.
- The lens is a featureless white square for ~30 shots. Even at "лампа гаснет" it just turns grey, which is the laziest asset in the film.
- Caption breaks mid-clause ("…ПРЯМО НА") on dramatic lines.
- The recap card at 19:21 is on screen for 3 s with 13 lines of small text: unreadable and purely decorative.
