## 10. Top 10 reusable techniques

1. **The format as fiction:** "N rules from a predecessor's notebook" turns the listicle into a creepypasta. The rules are diegetic, written in blue ballpoint on graph paper, then promoted to the rule card.
2. **Night chapter cards with a timeline** (НОЧЬ 1…7, ФИНАЛ), each titled after its threat ("МОКРЫЕ ДЕНЬГИ", "ФУРА БЕЗ ФАР").
3. **One workhorse set** (a counter in front view) reused for ≈40% of runtime, varied only by visitor, clock time, light state and tags. That makes it cheap to produce.
4. **The top-down floor-plan diagram** with a "ТЫ" dot, a yellow safety line and dotted footprints: spatial horror explained like a tactics board.
5. **A red digital wall clock** that updates every scene ("03:10", "03:26"), building tension with exact times.
6. **The lamp-flicker effect:** 4–6 frame on/off alternations for about 2 s, plus a 0.2 s white flash for a reveal.
7. **The CCTV quad screen:** a green monochrome with scanlines, CAM labels, a timestamp and a yellow focus box, with a silhouette appearing.
8. **Speech bubbles colour-coded by speaker** (pink border for the ghost child), plus giant floating head close-ups with a tear for emotion.
9. **Wrongness through palette only:** green-grey skin, a faceless visor, drips. It stays PG.
10. **A twist end plus a comment question that reuses the rule count** ("which rule would you break first?"), followed by the animated subscribe click.
