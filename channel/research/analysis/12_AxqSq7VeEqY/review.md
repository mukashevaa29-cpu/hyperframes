## 10. Top 10 reusable techniques

1. **A topic-skinned rule system:** a mute icon on every rule card, sonar rings on chapter cards and behind numerals. One motif ties all cards to the premise.
2. **A circular ending:** the video opens and closes on the same artefact (the job notice). The protagonist becomes the next victim and writes the notice.
3. **A found notebook with a missing page:** the rules come from a predecessor who vanished, and the last rule is the torn page revealed at the finale.
4. **A mute character speaking via a slate:** text-only dialogue that fits the no-lip-sync style perfectly.
5. **A traffic-light signal code** taught on screen (1 green / 2 yellow / 3 red flashes), then used in later scenes.
6. **Progress bars and timers as tension** ("тарелка разгоняется 1 минута", "задержка дыхания 10 сек", "город молчит ещё минуту").
7. **A noise-meter gauge prop** that reacts in scenes (needle drifts toward red) as an in-world danger indicator.
8. **Callout leader lines** to introduce a character's features ("СЕДАЯ / ОЧКИ / ВЯЗАНЫЙ ЖИЛЕТ").
9. **Dawn colour as release:** every 6:00 scene flips to a peach-violet sky; the night palette stays desaturated (saturation 86).
10. **Extreme element density on long static diagrams** (40+ pop-ins per shot) with counted objects (40 clocks, 12 lamps, 230 strings) shown as giant yellow numbers.
