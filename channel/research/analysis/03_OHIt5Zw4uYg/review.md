## 10. Top 10 reusable techniques

1. **A continuous escape route as the chapter spine** (car, tunnel, station, night, way up, home). Every rule is tied to the next physical obstacle.
2. **The count card in the hook** ("15 ПРАВИЛ" with a dot row and "every one was learned too late by someone").
3. **The rule-card breath:** the empty streaked stage for 0.3–0.6 s, then the numeral flies in with motion blur.
4. **The white-flash triplet** (3 × 0.2 s) plus a grey wash plus shake for violent impacts (electrocution, blast door).
5. **Diagram cutaways** (top-down train, rail cross-section, waveform, knock rhythm) built piece by piece over 12–20 s holds.
6. **Big tilted onomatopoeia** in caps ("ТОРМОЗ!", "ХРУСТЬ!", "БАМ!!!", "ЛЯЗГ") as sound design made visible.
7. **Palette-swapped template crowds;** infected marked with a red ring plus a single blood blob, which keeps it PG.
8. **Giant yellow counters** for tension (step counts, picket numbers).
9. **"Good news / bad news" paired tags** (green, then red) to flip expectations in the hook.
10. **A twist end via a phone UI** (an unknown-number message plus a door code typed from memory), then a question end card ("А ТЫ БЫ ОТКРЫЛ?") with "next story soon".
