## 8. Overlays, text & effects

- **Captions:**
  - **Type:** white heavy geometric caps (Montserrat Black-like), cap height ≈4.2% of frame height, baseline at y≈91%, centred, with a soft dark shadow and no box.
  - **Length:** 2–5 words per caption, phrase chunks, one line, never two. 42.7 changes/min.
  - **Colour:** whole-word colouring — red for "НЕ" / "НЕТ", yellow for the keyword, green for "УТРОМ", "ДНЁМ", "РАССВЕТА".
  - **No karaoke.**
- **Tags:** small rectangle stickers, cap height ≈1.8% of frame height, 2–5 words, tilted 0 to ±3°, with an overshoot pop-in.
  - **Colours:** white-on-black (neutral), red, green, yellow, blue, orange.
  - **Position:** placed near the object, often top-centre, stacked 2–3 deep ("НЕ ТОРМОЗИ / НЕ МИГАЙ ФАРАМИ / НЕ ОПУСКАЙ СТЕКЛО", 7:30).

## 10. Top 10 reusable techniques

1. **The 1.3-second rule card.** Empty streaked stage for 0.4 s, then a numeral with left motion blur, "ПРАВИЛО", a yellow tilted tag and the 20-dot row. Cut straight to the demo and read the imperative over the demo, not over the card. It works for dense lists (20 rules in 15 min).
2. **Chapter title riding on the rule card:** a yellow-outlined centred pill drops from above on the chapter's first rule instead of a separate chapter card.
3. **The location as an inset panel:** the recurring set (the RV cutaway) sits in a rounded rect at 96% × 72% on the dark stage, so the HUD and captions sit on black, never on art.
4. **Same art, day vs night:** reuse the interior with a ≈60% dark multiply, lamps off, and windows to navy-and-stars for "night".
5. **The found list with an empty last line:** a drivers list with years, then a red circle on the empty line 9. Pay it off at the end by filling line 9 with today's date and stamping "ПУСТАЯ" on line 10.
6. **Comparison pairs:** two side-by-side panels with a green check vs a red X (door vs window, clean RV vs boarded RV, birds vs no birds), each with a coloured tag.
7. **Hard-edged light cone:** a 25% white trapezoid from a small disc for the headlight. Use it for "a figure with no shadow".
8. **Dark speech bubbles** for voices outside, plus a red digital clock box for the time.
9. **Tags stacked 2–3 deep** as the narrator lists prohibitions (each pops on its word).
10. **CTA state change:** the red "ПОДПИСАТЬСЯ" pill is clicked by a cursor and turns into grey "ВЫ ПОДПИСАНЫ" plus a yellow bell with a ripple.

- The "too clean" petrol station blows out to near-white (luma spike) right after a dark stretch.
