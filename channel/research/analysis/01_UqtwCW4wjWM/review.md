## 9. Pacing

- **Pop-ins:** 38/min, a median of 4 per shot (0.62 per second of shot). Something new lands every ~1.6 s.
- **Captions:** change every ~1.35 s.
- **Cuts:** every ~8.8 s, nearly always on a sentence end.
- **Labels and narration:** labels appear on the noun as it is spoken (a tag names exactly the object the narrator mentions). Of 827 pop-ins, 801 fall within 0.35 s of a word onset; at 149 wpm that's loosely consistent with word-sync but not proof.
- **Rhythm:** within a rule segment it runs rule card (3–4 s), then 4–8 story/infographic shots, then the next rule card. Rules are about 60 s apart in the middle chapters.

## 10. Top 10 reusable techniques

1. **Persistent HUD:** a top-left chapter pill plus a top-right numbered progress-dot row (red = done), constant in every scene, makes long videos feel navigable.
2. **The rule card formula:** a giant red numeral with a bevel, "ПРАВИЛО", and a yellow tilted imperative tag on a dark streaked stage, with an impact push-in or shake and a 20-dot row under it.
3. **Chapter cards as a timeline** (first hour → year), with the active milestone in yellow. The structure is time-escalation, not topic.
4. **Second-person cold open:** a mundane specific moment, then a sensory loss, then a widening scale, then a twist ("not an accident, everywhere, a year"), then the counted promise ("20 rules"). About 80 s.
5. **Keyword-coloured captions:** yellow for names and places, red for danger, green for safety. Short 3–4 word chunks at 3.7% of frame height.
6. **Labels that pop on the spoken noun:** small tilted tags in a fixed colour code, about 38 per minute.
7. **Additive light only:** a translucent flashlight cone, radial candle/fire glows and one signature cyan glow, with no cast shadows, under a heavy vignette.
8. **The empty dark stage plus build-in** for every infographic beat: icons scale in with overshoot one by one.
9. **A recurring cast and a mystery thread** (apartment 13) seeded early and paid off in the finale, plus an unfinished "rule 21" cliffhanger used as the comment prompt.
10. **Animated subscribe end card:** the red pill gets cursor-clicked into a grey "subscribed" pill plus a yellow bell. The comment question repeats the video's promise.
