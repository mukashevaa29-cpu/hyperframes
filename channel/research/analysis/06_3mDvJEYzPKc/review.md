## 10. Top 10 reusable techniques

1. Clock-time chapter cards (red neon time + title + 6-node timeline) — chapters as hours of a
   night, which doubles as a ticking progress bar toward "08:00".
2. Rule cards as a handwritten note (tape, ruled paper, blue ink, red underline drawn on) next to
   a giant red numeral, landing with a 1-frame flash + shake, only 1.1-1.6 s on screen.
3. The found document as the spine: one physical list (13 points, one crossed out) re-shown at every
   rule and edited by the hero at the end ("you write a new line").
4. Geography tour in the cold open (labels + outline boxes + floor plan with every exit X'd) so
   later threats are spatially clear.
5. Light as the rule system: neon on/off, fridges' hum as camouflage, lights-out kinetic card.
6. Full-frame coloured strobe (blue ambulance light, 0.18 s toggles for 9 s) as a cheap,
   high-impact event.
7. Colour-coded tag language with an extra BLUE class for cold/sound/info.
8. Onomatopoeia typeset at the sound source.
9. Counters and diagrams that count with the narrator (shutter rotations, knocks, infected numbers,
   loudness bar chart).
10. Echo-trail sway on monsters + pure black silhouettes with one red detail for threats in the dark.
