## 6. Camera

Real shots: **64 % push-in** (145/228), 7 pull-outs, 14 pans, 24 shake-flagged (mostly slow handheld drift, shake
value 0.7–1.9). How the push works (measured):

- Total scale median **+4.9 %** (p10 +3.4, p90 +8.8, max ~+14 %).
- **Hold first, push second:** motion reaches 10 % of its travel at a median 50 % of the shot and 90 % at 79 %. So
  the shot sits still for half its length (while tags pop in), then pushes with an ease-out (109/135 classified
  ease-out) and lands about 1–2 s before the cut.
- Active speed median **1.8 %/s** (p10 0.75, p90 3.4 %/s). It is barely conscious: a felt "lean in", not a zoom.
- **Origin:** centre-middle is the most common (31), then bottom-right (25) and bottom-left (16), i.e. on the speaking
  character or the object the latest tag named, usually in the lower half. Pans are almost never used to compensate.
- **Chaining:** runs of up to 10 consecutive push-in shots. Each new shot starts at 1.0, so the cut resets to wide and
  the next shot pushes again: a saw-tooth zoom over the whole film. The rule and chapter cards are the only reliably static
  shots, and they read as a rest.

  The caption of the next line often fades in under the dissolve.
- A new shot opens with its main props already in place. Tags then build one by one, ~0.3–0.6 s apart.

## 9. Pacing

Something new about every 1.3 s (39 pop-ins/min + 45 caption changes/min + 8 shot changes/min). Typical
6–7 s shot: 0–0.4 s dissolve in, 3–5 tags pop on the nouns as they are spoken (popin median 4/shot, p90 9), the push
starts around mid-shot, and it dissolves out on the next sentence. Rule card plus rule restatement take about 6 s in all,
then the story moves straight on with a new day stamp in the HUD.

## 10. Top 10 reusable techniques

1. **Theme-matched chapter card** (money-green banknote with "НЕДЕЙСТВИТЕЛЬНО" stamp). Re-skin the card per episode
   topic.
2. **State-tracker HUD pill** (story clock + current inventory) next to the 20-dot rule progress.
3. **Hold-then-push**: static for the first ~50 % of the shot while labels land, then a 3–9 % ease-out push that settles
   before the cut. Reset to wide on every cut.
4. **4–6-frame micro-dissolve** as the default cut. It softens a dense edit without slowing it.
5. **Slide-wipe with white streak bars** reserved for rule and chapter cards.
6. **Cream ruled-paper rule card**: red circled numeral with ink spatter, dark-violet "ПРАВИЛО", dark-green rule slab.
   Keep it to ~1.5 s.
7. **Cold-open escalation chain**: one new consequence per shot every ~5 s, ending on a dated threat ("ДЕНЬ 9, 02:00,
   СТУК"), then credibility, agenda list, and an open loop on the final rule.
8. **Window grid as infographic** (lit windows recoloured to count households).
9. **Peephole fisheye and flashlight cones** for home-invasion tension without gore.
10. **CTA end card with a cursor clicking Subscribe** plus a "which rule matters most?" comment prompt that lists all rule
    numbers.

## Craft mistakes not to copy

- **Caption over the card UI:** on rule cards the caption sits on top of the bottom progress-dot row (rulecard7.jpg). Pick
  one of the two for the bottom band.
- **Rule card too short for its text:** a 4–6-word rule slab at ~3 % text height for 1.2–1.5 s cannot be read on a phone.
  The rule is only readable because it is repeated afterwards.
- **Label soup:** up to 6–8 tags on screen at ~2–3 % height (13:12 "РАДИО/ФОНАРИ/ЗАМКИ/ГЕНЕРАТОРЫ/НОЖИ/ЧИНЮ";
  11:36 the market tag wall). Tags overlap each other or signage ("СЪЕСТЬ" over "ПРОДУКТЫ" 11:12; "ТЫ ОДИН" over
  "ПРОДУКТЫ" 17:54) or are clipped by the frame edge ("КЕРОСИНОВАЯ ЛАМПА" 21:51).
- Tags that duplicate the caption word for word ("ТРАКТОР" / "ТРАКТОР СОСЕДА") add nothing.
- Captions broken by word count, not by phrase: they end on prepositions or half-names ("ПОЛУЧАЕТ СВОИ СЕРДЕЧНЫЕ КАПЛИ В",
  "НО ЗДЕСЬ ЭТОГО ПОЧТИ НЕ").
- Captions ghost through the micro-dissolves, so two caption lines are visible at once for 2–3 frames.
- HUD rule dots are ~15 px at 540p, illegible, and the HUD pills compete with the top tags.
- Cloned crowd rows with identical poses read as wallpaper. Vary pose, height and facing.
