## 10. Top 10 reusable techniques

1. **Cut a few frames before the sentence** (median −0.13 s) and **open on the empty stage**, then build. The cleanest cut
   discipline in the group.
2. **5.8 % → 100 % entry settle in 0.3 s** on every hard cut: a cheap "landing" that replaces camera moves.
3. **Built rule card**: numeral → "ПРАВИЛО" → tilted yellow slab → dot row, 3–4 s on a dark striped stage. The final rule
   gets a new colour ("ПРАВИЛО КУРЬЕРА" in yellow).
4. **Part cards with a 7-node timeline** plus a HUD pill naming the current part.
5. **"Previously on" recap** with its own pill, ending on the last episode's cliffhanger audio.
6. **Series-spanning CTA** (26 rule dots grouped by episode, "КТО ИЗ ШЕСТЕРЫХ ТЕБЕ БЛИЖЕ?").
7. **Rule-teaching diagram on a neutral dark stage** (eye test, timeline bar, dot map, stair zig-zag) between story plates.
8. **Rubber stamps** for verdicts ("ЖИВОЙ", "НИ РАЗУ", "«ПЕПЕЛ» ОТМЕНЁН").
9. **Silhouette threat**: black figures with white dot eyes, revealed by headlight bloom.
10. **Sunrise payoff palette** (salmon → peach sky, cream bloom) after 15 min of grey, which makes the ending feel earned.

## Craft mistakes not to copy

- The CTA rule dots (26 at ~1.2 % height) and the HUD dots are unreadable. The 7-node timeline labels are ~1 % height.
- The dark-stage opens sometimes last 0.7 s with nothing but a caption (rows 0049, 0081). Over 118 cuts it reads like
  dropped frames. Keep it to 6–8 frames, or show the plate faintly.
- Captions overlap characters' faces and diagrams on the lab set (the caption sits on the hand at 1:51 and on the box).
- Lab plate reused for ~35 % of the runtime with identical framing. Vary the angle and crop.
- Rule 8 card shown twice in a row (10:45 and 10:48): it looks like an edit error.
- Low saturation (62) plus a heavy grey vignette makes the lab scenes look muddy. The bright accents carry everything.
