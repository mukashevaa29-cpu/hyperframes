## DAY 1 · THE CARDS

On the first day, nobody takes the card seriously.

### RULE 1 | FILL EVERYTHING WITH WATER

Rule one.
On the first day, fill everything you have with [b:water].
The bathtub.
Every pot.
Every bottle.
When it gets cold enough, the pipes freeze, and the tap just stops.
Open the cabinet doors under the sinks, and let the taps drip a little at night.
Moving water freezes slower than still water.
Maya thinks it's a game.
She fills her rain boots.

### RULE 2 | LIVE IN ONE ROOM

Rule two.
Pick [g:one] room, and live in it.
You can't heat a whole house.
So you pick the living room, because it has the fireplace.
Towels under the doors.
Blankets over the windows.
Mattresses on the floor.
Every other room in the house becomes a place you visit quickly, wearing a coat.
A closed door keeps the heat where you are.

### RULE 3 | NEVER LET SNOW BURY THE VENTS

Rule three.
Never let the snow bury the [r:vents].
The furnace, the dryer, the water heater.
They all breathe through pipes in the outside wall.
When the snow covers them, the fumes come back inside.
You can't see it.
You can't smell it.
You just get very tired.
If anyone gets a headache, feels dizzy, or sick to their stomach all at the same time, get fresh air immediately.
Twice a day, you dig out the vent on the side of the house with a kitchen pot.
And there's something else on the card, under rule three, in smaller letters.
It says, the snow is not the only thing that's cold.

## DAY 3 · THE COLD

On the third day, the power goes out.

### RULE 4 | NEVER BURN FUEL INDOORS

Rule four.
Never use a grill, a generator, or a camping stove [r:inside].
Not in the house.
Not in the garage.
Not even with the door open.

### RULE 5 | STAY DRY

Rule five.
Stay [g:dry].
Cold doesn't kill you nearly as fast as wet.
Wet socks.
Wet gloves.
A shirt you sweated through while shoveling.
Wear layers, take one off before you start sweating, and change anything damp right away.

Wool is the best.

### RULE 6 | DON'T EAT THE SNOW

Rule six.
Don't [r:eat] the snow.
It feels like the obvious thing to do when the taps stop.

Melt it first.
Ten cups of snow make barely one cup of water, so melting it takes a lot of fuel.

Some of the snow isn't snow.
