## Description

It started on a Friday afternoon: the first real snow of the year. Then it didn't stop. Not that week. Not that month. Forty days.

On the first morning, a white plastic card was taped to every front door in town. Twenty rules in small black letters. Nobody knew who put them there. The people who followed them saw the spring.

Fill the bathtub. Live in one room. Count everyone every hour. Never follow a light in the whiteout. And if someone knocks and there's no snow on their shoulders, don't open the door.

Some of these rules are real winter advice. Fill containers with water before a storm, keep vents clear of snow, and never run a grill, generator or camping stove indoors or in a garage: carbon monoxide kills, and you can't see or smell it.
