# The Rule Stories — Shorts upload sheet

Files: `shorts/out/<id>.mp4` (1080×1920, 30 fps, 32–52 s, ≈ −14 LUFS). Rebuild with
`python3 tools/shorts.py shorts/shorts_spec.py [id ...]`.

## How to post

- One Short a day, best between 17:00 and 20:00 of the US East Coast (01:00–04:00 Moscow time); schedule them in Studio.
- In the Shorts editor set **Related video** to the full episode, so a "full story" link sits on the Short.
- Same settings as the long videos: not made for kids, **altered/synthetic content: Yes** (AI narration).
- Pin a comment with the link to the full video.
