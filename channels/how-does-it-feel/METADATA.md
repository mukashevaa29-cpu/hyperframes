# YouTube Shorts metadata

Title ≤ 100 characters, the brand phrase first. Put 3–5 hashtags in the description; the first three show above the title.
`#shorts` is optional, since vertical videos under 3 minutes are Shorts anyway. Alternative titles are for A/B tests.
