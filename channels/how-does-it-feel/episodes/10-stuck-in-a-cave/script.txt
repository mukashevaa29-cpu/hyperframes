How does it feel? When you get stuck upside down in a cave.
Utah. Nutty Putty Cave. 2009.
You squeeze into a narrow passage. You think it's a tunnel.
It's a dead end.
The gap is just twenty-five centimeters across. You slide in head first... and you're stuck.
Upside down. Arms pinned under your chest.
You can't go forward. You can't go back.
Blood floods your head. Every breath, the rock squeezes your ribs.
Your heart pumps against gravity. Hour after hour.
More than a hundred rescuers try to pull you out.
Then a rope anchor breaks. You slide back down.
Twenty-seven hours upside down.
Your heart gives out.
They sealed the cave with concrete. You're still inside.
And then... comes darkness.
