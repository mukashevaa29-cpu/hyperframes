How does it feel? When they lock you inside a bronze bull.
Sicily. The sixth century BC.
In front of you stands a life-size bull. Hollow. Cast in bronze.
A door in its side swings open. They shove you inside.
The door slams shut.
Dark. Cramped. It smells of metal.
Then they light a fire under its belly.
The first minutes are just warm.
You don't understand what's happening yet.
But bronze heats fast.
Within minutes, the walls are scorching. You can't touch them.
And there's nowhere left to stand.
The air turns hot, like an oven.
Every breath burns your lungs.
You find the only place you can still breathe. A pipe, leading into the bull's head.
You press your face against it... and scream.
But outside, no one hears a scream.
The pipes are built to turn your voice into the bellow of a bull.
The tyrant and his guests laugh.
To them, it's music.
Your voice breaks. The scream becomes a rasp.
The bull falls silent.
And then... comes darkness.
