How does it feel? When you fall into lava.
You slip at the edge of the crater.
Below you, a lake of molten rock. Twelve hundred degrees Celsius.
In the movies, you sink. In reality, you don't.
Lava is three times denser than your body. You land on top of it, like a mattress of fire.
Before you even touch it, the heat sets your clothes on fire.
The gas burns your throat. Sulfur. Every breath is acid.
The water in your body flashes into steam. Your skin blisters, then blackens.
You try to push yourself up. Your hands sink in, just a little. And stick.
You can't swim in it. You can't climb out of it.
Within seconds, you stop feeling anything.
And then... comes darkness.
