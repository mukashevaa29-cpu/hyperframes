How does it feel? When your car sinks into a river.
You lose control on a bridge. The car smashes through the railing.
You hit the water. The airbag explodes in your face.
For about a minute, the car floats.
Then water pours in around your feet. Freezing. Fast.
You push the door. It won't move.
The water outside presses on it with the weight of a horse.
You hit the window button. Nothing. The electrics are dead.
The water reaches your chest. Then your chin.
The car tips forward, engine first, into the dark.
You press your face to the roof and take one last breath.
Experts say there's one way out. Seatbelt off. Window open. Get out. In the first minute.
Nobody ever told you.
And then... comes darkness.
