How does it feel? When you're in Hiroshima the moment the atomic bomb goes off.
You're 500 meters from ground zero.
A flash.
The light is so bright it burns your retinas.
You don't even have time to blink.
Then comes the heat.
Your skin blisters... and bursts.
The patterns on your clothes are burned straight into your body.
One second later. The shock wave.
Your eardrums burst.
Glass tears into your skin in hundreds of shards.
And on the stone wall behind you, a shadow remains.
The shadow of the person you were a second ago.
And then... comes darkness.
