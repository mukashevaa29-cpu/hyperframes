How does it feel? When you're on the Titanic as it sinks.
April 15th, 1912. 2:20 in the morning.
The deck tilts beneath your feet.
There are lifeboats for barely half the people on board.
And most of them are already gone.
The stern rises out of the water. Then the ship breaks in two.
You jump.
The water is minus two degrees Celsius.
The cold hits like a thousand knives. You gasp, and swallow seawater.
You can't control your breathing.
Your life jacket keeps you afloat. But it can't keep you warm.
Your arms stop working. Then your legs.
Around you, fifteen hundred people are screaming in the dark.
Within twenty minutes, it goes quiet.
And then... comes darkness.
