How does it feel? When you wake up buried alive.
You open your eyes. Total darkness.
You reach up. Wood. Inches from your face.
You're in a coffin. Six feet under.
You scream. No one hears you.
A coffin holds about five hours of air. If you stay calm.
But you don't stay calm. You panic. You breathe faster.
Every breath fills the box with carbon dioxide.
Your head starts pounding. Your hands go numb.
You claw at the lid until your nails break.
In the eighteen hundreds, people feared this so much, they built coffins with a bell. A string ran to your hand, so you could ring for help.
You have no bell.
Your breathing slows. Your thoughts drift away.
And then... comes darkness.
