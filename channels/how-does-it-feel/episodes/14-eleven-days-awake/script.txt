How does it feel? When you don't sleep for eleven days.
San Diego, 1963. You're seventeen. And you want a world record.
No sleep. Not even a nap.
Day two. Your eyes stop focusing.
Day three. You get clumsy. Moody. Your thoughts slip away.
Day four. A street sign turns into a person. You're sure you're a famous football player.
Your brain starts shutting down in pieces, for seconds at a time, while your eyes are still open.
Day seven. Your hands tremble. Your speech slurs.
Day eleven. Two hundred and sixty-four hours awake. Your face goes blank. You can't finish a sentence.
You did it. The record is yours.
You finally close your eyes.
And then... comes darkness.
