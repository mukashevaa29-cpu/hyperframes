How does it feel? When you climb into Everest's death zone.
8,000 meters. Above this line, the human body starts dying.
Every breath brings in a third of the oxygen you're used to.
Your heart races, even when you're standing still.
Each step takes three, four, five breaths.
Fluid starts leaking into your lungs. Your cough begins to gurgle.
Then your brain begins to swell.
You forget why you're here. You hear voices that aren't there.
The cold eats your fingers. They turn white. Then black.
Some climbers suddenly feel warm, and take off their gloves.
You just want to sit down in the snow. For a moment. To rest.
More than three hundred people have died on this mountain. Many of them are still up there.
And then... comes darkness.
