How does it feel? When you're sucked out of a plane window.
June 10th, 1990. You're the captain of a British Airways jet, climbing over England.
At five kilometers, your windscreen blows out.
A mechanic fitted it with the wrong bolts. Eighty-four of them were too small.
The air rushes out of the cockpit, and takes you with it.
Your body is pulled through the window. Only your legs stay inside.
Outside, it's minus seventeen degrees. The wind hits you at over five hundred kilometers an hour.
A flight attendant grabs your legs, and holds on.
Your face is blue. Your eyes are open. The crew thinks you're dead.
But they don't let go.
For twenty minutes, until the plane lands.
You survive, with frostbite and broken bones. Five months later, you're flying again.
But out there, in that wind, the world goes quiet.
And then... comes darkness.
